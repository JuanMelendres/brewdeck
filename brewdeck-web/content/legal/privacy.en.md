*This is a translation. The Spanish version prevails.*

Parties (LFPDPPP, published in the DOF on 20 March 2025).

## 2. Personal data we process

| Category | Data |
|---|---|
| Identity and contact | Email address; display name (optional) |
| Authentication | Your password, stored only as an irreversible hash (Argon2id). We never see or store it in plain text. |
| Preferences | Language; light or dark theme |
| Content you record | Coffees, brew methods, recipes, brew sessions, ratings and notes |
| Technical data | IP address, browser, date and time, and the page or feature used, in the service's security logs |
| Session data | Session identifiers and single-use links (verification, password reset, email change), stored only as hashes |

**We do not process sensitive personal data or financial data.**

## 3. Purposes

**Primary purposes**, needed for the service:

1. Create and manage your account, and verify your email.
2. Let you log in and protect your account: sessions, attempt limits, password reset and email
   change.
3. Store and show you your content. If you choose to share a recipe, anyone with the link can see
   it too.
4. Send you account emails: verification, password reset, security notices and email change.
5. Prevent abuse, find faults and keep the service secure.
6. Meet legal obligations and answer requests from authorities.

**Secondary purposes:** none. We do not use your data for advertising, marketing or profiling, and
we do not sell it. If we add a secondary purpose in the future, such as a newsletter, we will ask
for your consent, and you can refuse without losing your account.

## 4. Consent

By creating your account after reading this notice, you accept the processing described for the
primary purposes (tacit consent, LFPDPPP Art. 7). You can revoke it at any time as described in
section 8. Revocation is not retroactive and means closing your account, because we cannot provide
the service without that data.

## 5. Providers that process data on our behalf

We use these providers to run BrewDeck. They process your data only on our instructions
(*personas encargadas*, LFPDPPP Art. 2), not for their own purposes:

| Provider | Service | Data location |
|---|---|---|
| Google Cloud (Google LLC) | Application server and technical logs | United States |
| Neon (Neon, Inc., on Amazon Web Services) | Database | United States |
| Vercel (Vercel Inc.) | Website | United States |
| Google (Gmail) | Account emails | United States |

Your data is stored outside Mexico. We do not transfer your data to third parties for their own
purposes, except when a competent authority orders it under the law.

## 6. Cookies and browser storage

We only use what the service needs to work and to remember your preferences:

| Name | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `brewdeck_refresh` | *httpOnly* cookie | Keep you signed in securely |
| `brewdeck-locale` | Cookie | Remember your language |
| Theme | Local storage | Remember the light or dark theme |

**We use no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies.**

## 7. Retention

- **Your account and all its content:** while the account exists. If you ask us to delete it, we
  delete it at once. *[Pending legal review: whether a blocking period is required, Art. 24.]*
- **Accounts that never verified their email:** deleted after 7 days.
- **Technical logs (IP and browser):** 30 days.
- **Expired or used single-use links:** deleted after 30 days.

## 8. ARCO rights and revoking consent

You can **access**, **rectify** or **cancel** your data, **object** to its processing, and
**revoke** your consent.

**From your account:**
- Rectify your name, language and theme on the account page.
- Change your email, confirmed from the new address.
- Download all your data as a JSON file (access).
- Delete your account and all its data at once, with your password (cancellation).

**By email** to jmelendresdev@gmail.com, with:
1. your name and your account email, so we can reply;
2. a document that proves your identity or, where applicable, your representative's;
3. a clear description of the data your request is about (not needed for access);
4. the right you are exercising and anything that helps us find your data.

**Deadlines (LFPDPPP Art. 31):**
- We reply within **20 business days** of receiving your request. The deadline can be extended
  once by the same period, if justified.
- If the request is granted, we carry it out within the following **15 business days**.
- It is free. Only shipping or copies could be charged, if you ask for them.

## 9. Limiting use or disclosure

- **Shared recipes:** you can stop sharing a recipe at any time, and the link stops working.
- **Account emails:** they are needed for security and cannot be turned off.
- **Any other use:** write to jmelendresdev@gmail.com.

## 10. Security

We apply administrative, technical and physical measures to protect your data, including:
- passwords hashed with Argon2id;
- encrypted connections (HTTPS);
- single-use links that expire;
- attempt limits;
- isolation of data between users.

If a breach significantly affects your rights, we will tell you by email at once, with what
happened and what you can do.

## 11. Minimum age

BrewDeck is only for people **18 or older**. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we
find an account of a minor, we will delete it.

## 12. Changes to this notice

We will publish any change on this page with its new version date. If the change is material, we
will also tell you by email and ask you to accept it when you log in.

## 13. Authority

If you believe your right to data protection was violated, you can go to the **Secretaría
Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno**, the authority under the LFPDPPP.
