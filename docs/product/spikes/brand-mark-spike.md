# Technical Spike: Brand Mark (Logo)

## 1. Summary

BrewDeck has no logo. The app shows a generic MUI coffee icon in a tile, and the browser tab still shows the
favicon generated by `create-next-app`. This spike compares three brand-mark concepts and defines how the chosen
one ships (SVG component, favicon, app icons).

- **Main question:** which mark represents BrewDeck and still reads at 16 px?
- **Expected decision:** one concept, refined, plus the file formats the app needs.

Origin: OQ-003 of the [design foundation TDD](../../architecture/design-foundation-tdd.md) (owner, 2026-10-05:
"no logo or brand yet, let's think about it").

## 2. Background

Repository facts (2026-10-05):

- `brewdeck-web/src/components/layout/AppShell.tsx` (`Logo`) and `src/components/auth/AuthLayout.tsx` render
  `LocalCafeIcon` from `@mui/icons-material` inside a `primary.main` tile.
- `brewdeck-web/src/app/favicon.ico` (16 and 32 px) is unchanged since the scaffold commit `0ab6ff7` (2026-07-03).
- `brewdeck-web/public/` holds the scaffold's `file.svg`, `globe.svg`, `next.svg`, `vercel.svg`, and `window.svg`.
  Nothing in `src/` references them.
- No `icon`, `apple-icon`, `manifest`, or `opengraph-image` file exists under `src/app/`.
- The recipe PDF (`src/lib/pdf/recipePdf.ts`) prints "BrewDeck" as plain text.
- Visual language: [ADR-014](../../decisions/ADR-014-ui-design-system-mui-theme-tokens.md). Light primary
  `#5B3A29` on `#FFF8EF`, dark primary `#D4A55A` on `#1A130E`, Fraunces display font, 12/18 px radii.

Concept canvas (private, owner access): <https://claude.ai/artifact/CAUzv49R1h2f6RjB7bonjc>. Each concept is shown
in both modes, at 128 px, as the sidebar lockup next to the "BrewDeck" wordmark, and at 48, 32, and 16 px.

## 3. Problem Statement

A generic icon gives the product no identity, and the scaffold favicon is the Next.js default. We need one mark
that fits the warm-café direction, works in light and dark tiles, and survives the 16 px favicon.

## 4. Goals

- Compare three concepts against explicit criteria.
- Pick one direction to refine.
- Define the delivery: React component, favicon, app icons, and where each one is used.

## 5. Non-Goals

- No full brand book, marketing site, or illustration set.
- No paid design work or trademark search (TODO if the product ever goes public).
- No change to the palette or fonts (ADR-014).

## 6. Key Questions

- Q-001: Which concept best says "coffee brewing" plus "deck" (a personal collection of recipes)?
- Q-002: Does it stay recognizable at 16 px?
- Q-003: Does one drawing work on both tiles (light espresso, dark gold) without a second version?
- Q-004: Which files does Next 16 need: `icon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-icon.png`, a manifest?

## 7. Constraints

- Drawn on a 24 × 24 grid with a 1.8 stroke and round caps, so it matches the MUI icons around it.
- Uses `currentColor`, so the theme decides its color (no color literals: TDD VR-003).
- Contrast: the mark colors are the palette's `primary` / `contrastText` pairs, already ≥ 8:1 in both modes.
- Fonts in an SVG must be converted to paths before shipping, so the mark does not depend on a loaded font.

## 8. Options Considered

| Option | Description | Pros | Cons | Complexity | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A · Cup and steam | Mug outline with a handle and two steam lines | Instantly reads as coffee; clean at 16 px | Generic: close to many coffee apps and to the current MUI icon | Low | Low distinctiveness |
| B · Bean on a deck | A filled card with a coffee bean, a second card fanned behind it | Tells both halves of the name: brew (bean) + deck (cards); ownable | Two layers get busy at 16 px; the back card nearly disappears | Medium | Medium legibility |
| C · Monogram "b" | Serif lowercase "b" (Fraunces) with two steam lines | Ties to the wordmark typeface; elegant | Reads as a letter more than coffee; the steam vanishes at 16 px; the glyph needs outlining | Medium | Medium recognizability |

## 9. Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Description | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meaning | Says coffee and, ideally, "deck" | High |
| Small-size legibility | Recognizable at 16 and 32 px | High |
| Distinctiveness | Not interchangeable with a stock icon | High |
| Fit with ADR-014 | Matches the warm-café look and Fraunces wordmark | Medium |
| Delivery cost | Hand-drawable SVG, no font dependency | Low |

## 10. Research Findings

### Finding 1: The current mark is a stock icon

`LocalCafeIcon` (Material Icons) appears in `AppShell` and `AuthLayout`. Any MUI app can show the same mark.

Impact: concept A must differ clearly from it, or it adds little.

### Finding 2: The favicon and public assets are scaffold leftovers

Facts in §2. Impact: whatever is chosen, the favicon and unused `public/` SVGs should be replaced or removed in
the same delivery.

### Finding 3: Next 16 file conventions cover the icons

Verified in the bundled Next 16.3.6 docs (`01-app/03-api-reference/03-file-conventions/01-metadata/`):
`app/favicon.ico` (root only), `app/icon.(ico|png|svg)`, and `app/apple-icon.(png|jpg)` are linked
automatically, with no `<head>` code. `app/manifest.ts` returning `MetadataRoute.Manifest` serves the web app
manifest. There is no `middleware`/`proxy` file, so the manifest and icons are public.

### Finding 4: Expected small-size behavior (not yet reviewed)

Assumption, based on the drawings' geometry (a 1.8 stroke on a 24 grid is about 1 px at 16 px). Confirm in the
canvas review:

- A should be the most legible at 16 px.
- B carries the most meaning; at 16 px the bean should still read, the back card barely.
- C's steam lines likely disappear below 32 px, leaving a "b" in a tile.

## 11. Proof of Concept Plan

- **Built:** the concept canvas (§2). No code.
- **Next, once a concept is picked:** one refinement round in the canvas (stroke weight, simplified 16 px variant
  if needed), then the delivery in §17.
- **Time-box:** 1 session for refinement, 1 PR for delivery.

## 12. Proof of Concept Results

Canvas published 2026-10-05 with the three concepts in both modes and at four sizes. Owner review: A liked but
"too generic"; B chosen.

Refinement round (same canvas, 2026-10-05):

- **Simple variant** for 16 px (and optionally 32 px): front card and bean only, bean enlarged, heavier stroke.
- **Wordmark weights:** option 1 = "Brew" 700 + "Deck" 400; option 2 = "Brew" 300 + "Deck" 700. Both Fraunces.
- **Home screen:** the mark on an iOS rounded tile and on Android circle and squircle crops (maskable icon).

## 13. Trade-Off Analysis

| Trade-Off | Benefit | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meaning vs legibility (B vs A) | B tells the product's story | Needs a simplified favicon variant |
| Typographic vs pictorial (C vs A/B) | C is consistent with the wordmark | Weaker "coffee" signal |
| One drawing vs a 16 px variant | Single source of truth | Small sizes may blur |

## 14. Risks

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mark illegible at 16 px | Unrecognizable tab icon | Medium (B, C) | Simplified favicon variant (for B: drop the back card) |
| Resembles an existing coffee brand | Confusion, legal exposure if public | Low (personal project) | Quick visual search before final; trademark check only if the app goes public |
| Font-based glyph renders differently | Broken mark | Medium (C) | Outline the glyph to paths |

## 15. Recommendation

Recommended option: **B · Bean on a deck**, with a simplified 16 px favicon (front card and bean only).

- It is the only concept that says what BrewDeck is: brewing, plus a personal deck of recipes and coffees.
- A is the safest at small sizes but too close to the stock icon it would replace (Finding 1).
- C is elegant but reads as a letter, and its distinctive part (the steam) vanishes at small sizes.
- Assumption: the owner values meaning over the absolute simplest shape. If not, A is the fallback.

Validate next: the owner's review of the canvas, then the simplified 16 px variant.

## 16. Decision

- Decision: **B · Bean on a deck**, with the simple variant at 16 px. Wordmark in Fraunces with two weights
  (option 1: "Brew" 700 + "Deck" 400, chosen 2026-10-05). The app is installable on a phone home screen.
- Date: 2026-10-05
- Owner: Juan (product owner)
- Status: Accepted. Delivered on branch `feat/web-brand-mark`.
